Carlito, Arimo and Tinos, metric-compatible with Calibri, Arial and Times New Roman, so a resume laid out with them
takes the same space when opened in Word. Each is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see the *-LICENSE.txt
files). Files come from the @fontsource packages (latin and latin-ext subsets) and are served by the app and read by
the PDF export, so the editor and the PDF use identical fonts.
